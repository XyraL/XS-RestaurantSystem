# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

The first release, as a beta.

- Builder with 11 styles, places, menu, recipes and map blip
- 8 themes per business, from paper tickets to a neon bar
- See every screen full size from the builder before saving
- Registers with a pay prompt on the customer's own screen
- Kitchen and pickup screens on the wall
- Cooking stations with 7 minigames
- Pickup counter that hands the food over
- Payments into the business account
- Menu boards on the wall
- Staff can mark items sold out from the register
- Self-order kiosks, only while someone is working
- Drive-thru with an intercom through pma-voice
- Seats anyone can sit on
- Food app for pickup and delivery, or /food without a phone
- Staff deliveries with GPS to the customer
- Office laptop with sales, prices, sold out and the boss menu
- Bosses can change prices within a set range
- Business account in the office for the ranks you pick
- Supply orders by van to the back door or from the warehouse
- Discord logs per business or for the whole server
- Points can be placed with or without a prop
